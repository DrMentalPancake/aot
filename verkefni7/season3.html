Attack on Titan Season 3 shifts the focus from battles with Titans to the darker, hidden conflicts within humanity itself. After the events of Season 2, Eren Yeager and his friends return to the walls with more questions than answers. The Survey Corps begins uncovering the government’s corruption, learning that the royal family ruling within Wall Sina is not the true lineage of power. Commander Erwin Smith, determined to reveal the truth about humanity’s past, risks everything in a dangerous plan to overthrow the fake monarchy and restore the real royal bloodline.

Meanwhile, Eren continues to struggle with his new Titan abilities, particularly the mysterious Coordinate power that allows him to control other Titans. At the same time, Historia Reiss—previously known as the gentle and secretive Krista—discovers her true identity as the legitimate heir to the throne. Her connection to the royal family makes her central to Erwin’s rebellion. When the Survey Corps is declared an enemy of the state, Levi and his squad go into hiding while being pursued by the ruthless Kenny Ackerman, a deadly fighter and the leader of the government’s Anti-Personnel Control Squad. He also happens to be Levi’s uncle, revealing part of Levi’s tragic past and the violent history of the Ackerman bloodline.
Attack On Titan Historia GIFfrom Attack On Titan GIFs

As Levi’s squad protects Eren and Historia, they are captured by Rod Reiss, Historia’s father, who reveals shocking truths: the Titans were created by humans, and the royal family has long held the power to erase people’s memories. Eren’s Titan abilities are revealed to have come from his father, Grisha Yeager, who stole the Founding Titan power from the Reiss family before passing it on to Eren. Rod Reiss attempts to turn Historia into a Titan so she can reclaim this power, but she rejects her father’s manipulation. In a dramatic confrontation, Rod transforms into a grotesque, gigantic Titan and crawls toward Orvud District. Eren and the Survey Corps devise a plan to destroy him, and Historia herself delivers the killing blow—later being crowned the true Queen of the Walls.
Attack On Titan Aot GIFfrom Attack On Titan GIFs

With the corrupt monarchy overthrown, humanity gains new hope and a sense of unity. But the story soon turns outward again as the Survey Corps prepares for their long-awaited mission to retake Wall Maria and reach the basement in Eren’s old home—where Grisha’s secrets are said to lie. The second half of the season delivers some of the most intense action in the series: the Battle of Shiganshina District. The Survey Corps faces Reiner (the Armored Titan), Bertolt (the Colossal Titan), and the mysterious Beast Titan in an all-out war. The fight is devastating—many soldiers die, including Commander Erwin, who leads a suicidal charge against the Beast Titan to give Levi a chance to attack.
Erwin Smith Charge GIFfrom Erwin Smith GIFs

In the end, Levi nearly kills the Beast Titan’s human form, Zeke Yeager, but he escapes. Meanwhile, Eren and Armin manage to defeat Bertolt, but Armin is burned alive in the process. Using the Titan injection, Levi must choose between saving Erwin or Armin. After a painful debate, they save Armin, who inherits Bertolt’s power and becomes the new Colossal Titan.
Armin Colossal Titan GIFfrom Armin GIFs

Finally, Eren, Mikasa, and Armin reach the basement of Eren’s old house, where they uncover journals left by Grisha Yeager. These reveal the truth: the world beyond the walls still exists, and humanity is not extinct. The people within the walls are descendants of a nation called Eldia, exiled to the island of Paradis and trapped there by an opposing civilization known as Marley. The Titans are weapons born from this ancient conflict, and the walls were built to hide the Eldians from the rest of the world.
The season ends with Eren and his friends standing on the shore, seeing the ocean for the first time—realizing that beyond it lies their true enemy.
