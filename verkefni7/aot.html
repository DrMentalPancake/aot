Info about Attack on Titan(shingeki no kyojin)


Attack on Titan, known in Japan as Shingeki no Kyojin, is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Hajime Isayama. The story first appeared in September 2009 when it began serialization in Kodansha’s Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine. Over the course of more than a decade, the manga unfolded through 139 chapters, eventually concluding in April 2021 with a total of 34 collected volumes. From the beginning, its mix of dark fantasy, mystery, and intense survival themes caught readers’ attention, and it quickly rose to become one of the most successful manga series in history.

The anime adaptation followed a few years later, debuting in 2013. Produced initially by Wit Studio, the first three seasons delivered breathtaking animation, suspenseful storytelling, and a faithful adaptation of the manga’s unfolding mysteries. For the final season, the production was handed over to MAPPA, another major Japanese animation studio, which carried the story to its conclusion in 2023. Over its ten-year run, the anime captivated audiences worldwide and turned the series into a global cultural phenomenon.

Set in a world where humanity hides behind enormous walls to protect themselves from man-eating giants known as Titans, Attack on Titan(shingeki no kyojin) combines thrilling action with deep philosophical questions. While at first it presents itself as a simple tale of humans versus monsters, the series gradually reveals layers of political intrigue, hidden history, and moral complexity. Its themes revolve around freedom, oppression, the cost of survival, and the often harsh realities of human nature.

The impact of Attack on Titan(shingeki no kyojin) cannot be overstated. By the time the manga ended, it had sold more than 120 million copies worldwide, ranking it among the best-selling manga of all time. The anime drew in millions of viewers across the globe, often praised for its stunning animation quality, epic soundtrack, and emotionally powerful storytelling. Beyond Japan, it helped expand the popularity of anime internationally, becoming a gateway series for many new fans.
