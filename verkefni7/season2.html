Attack on Titan(shingeki no kyojin) Season 2 picks up immediately after the shocking discovery of a Titan’s face hidden inside the walls. The Survey Corps and the military scramble for answers, but before they can act, a new crisis emerges: Titans are somehow found inside Wall Rose with no visible breach in the structure. Soldiers are sent out to investigate while villagers within the territory are evacuated. During this mission, Conny Springer finds his village destroyed and discovers a Titan that eerily resembles his mother, hinting that Titans may be far closer to humanity than previously believed.

As the investigation unfolds, a terrifying new enemy appears: the Beast Titan, a towering creature covered in fur, capable of speech and of commanding other Titans. His presence shows that the Titans are evolving in intelligence and coordination. Meanwhile, several of the Survey Corps recruits, including Ymir and Krista (later revealed as Historia Reiss), are forced into a desperate battle when they are surrounded by Titans. In a shocking turn, Ymir reveals her ability to transform into a Titan, saving her comrades but also deepening the mystery of how humans can become Titans.

The most devastating revelation of the season comes when Reiner Braun, in a moment of cold confession, reveals to Eren that he and Bertolt Hoover are secretly the Armored Titan and Colossal Titan—the very monsters responsible for the fall of Wall Maria and the destruction of Eren’s home five years earlier. They attempt to kidnap Eren and Ymir, triggering a fierce confrontation. The Survey Corps launches a desperate rescue mission, leading to a massive battle between Eren and Reiner in their Titan forms, while Bertolt uses his Colossal Titan to devastating effect.
Reiner Bertholdt GIFfrom Reiner GIFs

In the chaos, Hannes, the soldier who once saved Eren and Mikasa as children, sacrifices himself trying to save them again, only to be killed by the same Titan that ate Eren’s mother. Overcome with grief and rage, Eren unlocks a new ability called the “Coordinate,” which allows him to command Titans. Using this newfound power, he rallies nearby Titans to attack Reiner and Bertolt, forcing them to retreat and narrowly saving the Survey Corps from annihilation.

The season ends with humanity shaken once more. The truth that humans themselves can transform into Titans, that enemies have been hiding in plain sight, and that Eren now possesses a mysterious power that could turn the tide of war sets the stage for the battles and revelations to come. Meanwhile, in the shadows, the Beast Titan and his unknown human controller prepare for the next move, hinting that an even greater war lies ahead.
