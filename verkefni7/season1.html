Attack on Titan(shingeki no kyojin) Season 1 begins in Shiganshina District, where humanity lives behind massive walls that have kept them safe from Titans for over a century. This fragile peace is shattered when the Colossal Titan, taller than the walls themselves, suddenly appears and smashes open the gate to Wall Maria. Soon after, the Armored Titan charges through the inner gate, allowing countless Titans to flood in. During the chaos, young Eren Yeager witnesses his mother being eaten alive after being trapped under rubble. This moment shapes his destiny, driving him to swear that he will one day wipe out every Titan. Alongside his adoptive sister Mikasa Ackerman and childhood friend Armin Arlert, Eren joins the military to fight back.

Five years later, after harsh training, Eren and his comrades graduate as soldiers. The peace is broken again when the Colossal Titan reappears at Trost District and breaches Wall Rose. In the brutal defense, many soldiers die, and Eren himself sacrifices his life to save Armin, only to reemerge with the shocking ability to transform into a Titan. This discovery terrifies the military but also gives humanity new hope. Under the command of Captain Levi and Commander Erwin Smith of the Survey Corps, Eren is placed under supervision. Using his Titan form, he helps lift a massive boulder to seal the Trost breach, marking humanity’s first major victory against the Titans.

With Eren now a crucial weapon, the Survey Corps launches an expedition outside the walls. There, they encounter the Female Titan, a deadly, intelligent foe who slaughters soldiers while relentlessly pursuing Eren. After a desperate struggle, she is revealed to be Annie Leonhart, one of their former cadet comrades. Eren fights her in a devastating battle across Stohess District, eventually defeating her, but Annie encases herself in a hardened crystal, sealing away her secrets.

The season ends with a chilling revelation: hidden within the very walls that protect humanity lies the face of a colossal Titan, raising terrifying questions about the true nature of the world, the Titans, and the history humanity has been told.
